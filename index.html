Our Story

A husband and wife,
a small UK workshop

LuxeScent began with a simple frustration: everything on the market either smelled cheap or looked it. So we started blending our own — small batches, proper glass, fragrance oils chosen because we wanted them in our own car.

Everything is still made by the two of us, by hand, in the UK. Minimal design, long-lasting scent, and a touch of everyday luxury.

Questions

Good to know

How long does the fragrance last?

Six to eight weeks with normal use. Heat speeds evaporation, so in summer tighten the cap when you park — it'll stretch the life considerably.

Are these the actual designer perfumes?

No. They're our own blends, made from high-quality fragrance oils chosen for their similarity in character to well-known perfumes. We're independent and not connected to any designer house.

Will it leak or stain my interior?

The cap seals for transit. Keep it upright when hanging and avoid contact with plastics and painted trim — fragrance oil can mark some surfaces.

Can I refill it?

Refills are on the way. Register your interest below and we'll let you know first.

How fast is delivery?

Free UK delivery on every order, dispatched from our workshop. Orders currently arrive within a few working days — see the Etsy listing for live estimates.

Do you offer returns?

Because these are hand-blended fragrance products we can't accept returns, but if anything arrives damaged or wrong, message us and we'll put it right.
