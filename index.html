Our Story

A husband and wife,
a small UK workshop

LuxeScent began with a simple frustration: everything on the market either smelled cheap or looked it. So we started blending our own — small batches, proper glass, fragrance oils chosen because we wanted them in our own car.

Everything is still made by the two of us, by hand, in the United Kingdom. Minimal design, long-lasting scent, and a touch of everyday luxury.

Questions

Good to know

How long does the fragrance last?

Six to eight weeks with normal use. Heat speeds evaporation, so in summer tighten the cap when you park — it stretches the life considerably.

Are these the actual designer perfumes?

No. They are our own blends, made from high-quality fragrance oils chosen for their similarity in character to well-known perfumes. We are independent and not connected to any fragrance house.

Will it leak or stain my interior?

The cap seals for transit. Keep it upright when hanging, and avoid contact with plastics and painted trim — fragrance oil can mark some surfaces.

Clear or smoked glass — what is the difference?

Purely aesthetic. Clear shows the oil level; smoked glass conceals it and reads more discreet against a dark interior.

Can I refill it?

Refills are on the way. Register your interest below and we will let you know first.

How quickly do orders arrive?

Complimentary UK delivery on every order, dispatched from our workshop. See the Etsy listing for live estimates.

Do you accept returns?

Because these are hand-blended fragrance products we are unable to accept returns, but if anything arrives damaged or incorrect, message us and we will put it right.
